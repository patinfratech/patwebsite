    What helps us quote

    • Drawings

      Plans or sketches with dimensions, in any format. Attach them to the email when it opens.

    • Site location

      The village and taluk, a survey number or a map pin, and how a lorry reaches the site.

    • Target date

      When you want work to start, and when you need to move in or open.

    • Budget range

      A range is enough. It tells us whether to price one full scope or a few options.

    What happens after you send it

    1. A reply by email

      We read your brief and reply by email, with any questions and a time to see the site.

    2. A site visit

      We walk the site with you to see access, levels and ground conditions, and agree what you need.

    3. A written scope

      You get the scope in writing: what is included and what is not. The programme, estimate and method statement follow before any work starts.
