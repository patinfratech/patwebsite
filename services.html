Services

Industrial buildings

Factories, warehouses, processing plants and pre-engineered steel structures, planned around the equipment and material flow they have to carry.

  • Pre-engineered and conventional steel buildings
  • Heavy-duty floors and machine foundations
  • Utilities, yards and internal roads

Data centres

Mission-critical facilities where structure, power, cooling and security are coordinated from the first drawing so the building can run without interruption.

  • Core and shell construction
  • Raised floors, plant rooms and utility blocks
  • Coordination of civil works with power and cooling

Residential

Individual homes, apartments and housing developments built to a clear specification, with finishes and services checked before handover.

  • Independent houses and villas
  • Apartment and group housing blocks
  • Structure, finishing and building services

Commercial

Offices, retail, hospitality and institutional buildings delivered to open on time and to operate efficiently afterwards.

  • Office and retail buildings
  • Educational and healthcare buildings
  • Interior fit-out and facade works

Land and site development

Turning raw land into serviced, buildable plots: earthworks, drainage, utilities and access, set out accurately from survey data.

  • Earthworks, grading and compaction
  • Storm water, sewer and water networks
  • Layout roads, compound walls and landscaping

Roads and highways

Rural, urban and industrial roads built on properly prepared sub-grades, with drainage and pavement layers placed to specification.

  • Flexible and rigid pavements
  • Culverts, drains and road furniture
  • Widening, strengthening and resurfacing

Bridges

Road and pedestrian bridges, flyovers and cross-drainage structures, from foundations through to deck and finishing.

  • Foundations, piers and abutments
  • Girders, deck slabs and bearings
  • Approaches and protection works

Tunnels and underground works

Underpasses, utility tunnels, basements and other underground structures where ground conditions and water control govern the method.

  • Cut-and-cover structures and underpasses
  • Deep excavation and ground support
  • Waterproofing and drainage

General construction

All other civil and building work, from a single structure to a full turnkey package with design coordination, procurement and execution.

  • Turnkey and item-rate contracts
  • Renovation, extension and retrofitting
  • Project and construction management

How a project runs

Five stages, one accountable team.

  1. Understand

    We study your requirement, the site and the constraints, and agree the scope in writing.

  2. Plan

    Survey, design coordination, method, programme and budget are worked out before mobilisation.

  3. Build

    Work proceeds to the programme with the right equipment and supervised, trained crews.

  4. Verify

    Materials and workmanship are tested and inspected at each stage, and results are recorded.

  5. Hand over

    You receive the finished work with as-built records and the documents needed to operate it.
