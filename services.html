Four kinds of building, from a steel shed to an apartment block, each with its typical scope and what we need from you to quote.

Industrial buildings

Factories, warehouses, processing plants and pre-engineered steel structures, planned around the equipment and material flow they have to carry.

Typical scope
  • Pre-engineered and conventional steel buildings
  • Heavy-duty floors and machine foundations
  • Utilities, yards and internal roads
What we need from you
The site location, the machines and loads the floor must carry, and any layout or drawings you have.

Contract types

Any of the services above can be let on one of these three contracts. We agree which one in writing before work starts, alongside the four documents you hold us to.

  • Turnkey

    One contract covers design coordination, procurement and construction. You deal with one party and receive the finished works.

    Suits an owner who wants one point of responsibility.

  • Item-rate

    You supply the drawings and the bill of quantities. We price each item, and payment follows the quantities measured on site.

    Suits a tender, or an owner whose design is complete.

  • Construction and project management

    You keep your own contractors and suppliers. We plan, coordinate and supervise the work for you and report progress against the programme.

    Suits an owner who already has contractors in place.
