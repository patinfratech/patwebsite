Who we are

A construction company designed around technology from day one.

Most construction firms add technology to old ways of working. We started the other way round. PAT Infratech was founded to use the best available machinery, tools and AI at every stage, from survey and planning to execution and handover.

The result we are working towards is simple to state: structures that are built accurately, safely and on schedule, with the client able to see progress and cost at any time.

How we work

Modern machinery, digital models and AI on every project.

  1. AI-assisted planning

    Schedules, sequences and resource plans are tested against many scenarios before work starts, so clashes and delays are found on screen instead of on site.

  2. Digital building models

    Structure, services and finishes are coordinated in a shared 3D model. Quantities, drawings and site instructions all come from the same source.

  3. Survey by drone and laser

    Aerial and laser surveys give an accurate picture of the ground before design, and a measured record of progress during construction.
