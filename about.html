A new company, set up to build with modern methods from day one.

PAT Infratech (OPC) Private Limited is a construction company in Namakkal District, Tamil Nadu. It takes on buildings and infrastructure: factories, data centres, homes, offices, roads, bridges and tunnels.

Why we started

Construction shapes how people live, work and move. Much of it still runs on methods that waste time, material and money. PAT Infratech was set up to work differently.

The company is registered in Kumarapalayam Taluk, Namakkal District. It takes on industrial buildings, data centres, residential and commercial projects, land development, roads, bridges, tunnels and general civil works.

Every project will start with a measured survey of the site. The building will be modelled before material is ordered, and the work will be checked against the drawings as it goes up. That is what lets a builder put a date and a cost in writing, and keep to both.

Our aim and mission

To become one of the most technologically advanced, AI-oriented infrastructure companies in the world.

Our mission is to deliver infrastructure of lasting quality, combining sound engineering with modern machinery, digital tools and artificial intelligence.

Four working rules

Rules you can hold us to on every project, from the first site visit to handover.

  • Variations go in writing before the work, not after.

    If the scope or the price has to change, you see the change in writing and agree it before anyone builds it.

  • We build to the drawing and the code.

    When something on site does not match the drawing, we stop, tell you, and put it right before going on.

  • Work stops when it is not safe.

    No date and no saving is worth an injury. A task that cannot be done safely waits until it can.

  • Every estimate is itemised.

    Quantities and rates are listed line by line, so you can check them against the drawings and compare them with other bids.
