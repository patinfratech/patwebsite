How a project runs

The same five stages on every job, large or small. The 3D model and digital QA are planned, not yet in use; the technology page sets out what each will do.

  1. Understand

    We visit the site, survey it and agree in writing what you need, by when and for how much.

    Survey

  2. Plan

    Drawings, quantities, method and programme are worked out before anyone mobilises.

    3D model

  3. Build

    Work follows the agreed method and programme, with a written record for each day on site.

  4. Verify

    Levels, lines and materials are checked against the drawings, and every result is recorded.

    Digital QA

  5. Hand over

    You receive the finished work with its as-built drawings and test results.

Four documents you get before we start

You receive all four in writing, and work on site begins once you have agreed them.

  1. Scope

    What is included, what is excluded, and who supplies which materials.

  2. Programme

    The order of work, the dates, and what each date depends on.

  3. Estimate

    Quantities and rates, item by item, so you can compare it with any other quote.

  4. Method statement

    How each part will be built, with which equipment, and how it will be checked.
