How a project runs

Survey first, then plan, build and check. The same order on every job, from a compound wall to a factory.

  1. Survey the site

    We walk the ground with you and take its levels.

    You receive the levels of your site, taken from a fixed benchmark.

  2. Plan on paper

    Drawings, quantities, method and dates are fixed before any machine arrives.

    You receive the scope, programme, estimate and method statement.

    3D building models: planned

  3. Build to the method

    Each day on site gets a written record.

    You receive the daily site record, as the work goes.

  4. Check against the drawings

    Levels, lines and materials are tested, and the results kept.

    You receive the test results for each check.

    Digital QA and safety records: planned

  5. Hand over with records

    You get the finished work, as-built drawings and test results.

    You receive the as-built drawings and every test result.

Four documents you agree before work starts

Each one is yours to hold us to.

  • Scope

    What is included, what is not, and who supplies which material.

  • Programme

    The order of work, the dates, and what each date depends on.

  • Estimate

    Quantities and rates, item by item, ready to compare with any other quote.

  • Method statement

    How each part is built, with which equipment, and how it is checked.

All four are drawn up from your brief. See what helps us quote, then send the brief from the Contact page.
