What each method is for

AI-assisted planning Planned
Programmes, sequences and resource plans are tested against several scenarios before work starts, so clashes and delays show up on screen first.
Digital building models Planned
Structure, services and finishes are coordinated in one 3D model. Quantities, drawings and site instructions all come from that model.
Drone and laser survey Planned
The ground is measured before design starts, and progress is measured again during construction.
Modern machinery Planned
Equipment is chosen for accuracy and output: mechanised concreting and system formwork that shorten cycle times.
Quality and safety monitoring Planned
Inspection records, material test results and safety observations are kept digitally and reviewed for repeated problems.
Cost control Planned
Estimates, purchases and site consumption are tracked together, so you and the site team see the same numbers.

Where AI is used, and where an engineer decides

AI checks the programme. An engineer signs it.

What AI will check

  • The programme, for clashes, gaps and sequences that cannot be built
  • Quantities in the model against the estimate
  • Inspection and test records, for defects that repeat

What an engineer decides

  • Signs the programme and the method statement
  • Approves every structural and safety decision
  • Issues every instruction to the site

Like every method on this page, AI-assisted planning is marked Planned. Once it is in use, nothing it produces goes to site without an engineer’s signature.

Why better information matters on site

Most problems on a construction site begin as information problems: a drawing that does not match the ground, a delivery that arrives out of sequence, a defect found too late. Each one costs time and money to correct.

A measured survey, a coordinated model and a tested programme remove many of these before work starts. Recording what happens on site then makes the next project easier to predict.

For you, the result is fewer surprises, progress you can see, and finished works that match the design.
